01 / STRUCTURE

Begin with what is known.

Collect client-confirmed work, real photographs and intended reading tasks. Establish a complete page before adding the motion system.

The unanimated document is the foundation, not a failure state.

02 / STATE

Connect the evidence.

One active chapter identifier drives the visual stage and its descriptive label. Reversing scroll must restore the preceding stage without divergent state.

Content is never fabricated to make the scroll animation look impressive.

03 / FALLBACK

Preserve a readable path.

A narrow viewport turns the visual stage into a static figure. The full sequence remains keyboard-accessible and the reader can always leave the scene.

Nothing important disappears when JavaScript is unavailable.
