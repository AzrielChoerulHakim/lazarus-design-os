# Visual DNA — Sculptural Impasto

## Identity thesis

A world that cannot exist is painted as an object that could: physically convincing masses of oil pigment form impossible architecture, landscapes and material transitions. The **material of the painting** and the **impossible relationship between forms** must both be unmistakable.

### Seven observed visual coordinates (qualitative)

| Axis | Direction | Fails when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medium | Thick oil paint sculpted by knife and bristle | Looks like smooth CGI, generic watercolor, a flat vector or a noisy overlay |
| Pigment | Deep indigo/ultramarine, mineral bone, sienna, umber and selectively lit ivory | Every element has equally saturated digital color |
| Texture | Ridge direction follows an object and its implied motion; relief shadows are localized | Random embossing or uniform brush texture coats everything |
| Contradiction | One dominant irrational relationship (a colonnade liquefying, a sea suspended above steps) | Only extra moons/statues/objects without conceptual transformation |
| Depth | Clear near/middle/far structure and atmospheric separation | High detail and sharpness everywhere |
| Lighting | Coherent world-source plus coherent raking light over raised paint | Shadows point in incompatible directions without editorial reason |
| Composition | Authored focal point, asymmetry and deliberate readable quiet zones | Busy wallpaper behind important copy |

## The painting must not be reduced to an effect

Do not create a normal-looking image and add a digital impasto filter as a finishing touch. Paint ridge relief is part of the shape itself: mineral faces, folded stone, clouds and pigment ribbons should follow readable material transformations. Some planes may be flatter to maintain hierarchy and visual breathing room.

## Artistic references versus imitation

The selected conceptual family is Surrealism; the characteristic paradox of Salvador Dalí was discussed as a reference to **reasoning about irrational forms**, not an instruction to duplicate his clocks, figures, signatures or named compositions. The thick-paint execution comes from impasto technique, not from a claim that Dalí painted all works with heavy impasto.

## Negative examples / immediate rejection

- A portal, statue, moon and flying cloth placed independently without a conceptual relationship.
- A realistic statue screenshot whose only artistic addition is noisy surface texture.
- A hero assembled from separate image cutouts, SVG paths, or CSS-generated lighting.
- An identical dream landscape used for a lawyer, researcher and illustrator without justification.
- Generic luxury serif heading and tiny mono metadata that obscure the actual CV.
- Any non-authored client claim, pretend case study, invented project dates or hidden contact.
