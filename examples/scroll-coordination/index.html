Read the method, not the motion.

Scroll can coordinate context, but all four fictional stages remain in a normal, accessible document. No invented metrics, scroll lock or mandatory animation.

A documented process

  1. 01 / Intake

    Collect

    Identify real source materials before introducing visual interpretation. Mark missing documents instead of completing the story with invention.

  2. 02 / Qualification

    Verify

    Separate sourced claims from hypotheses. A compelling visual sequence cannot upgrade an uncertain claim into evidence.

  3. 03 / Composition

    Arrange

    Set the reading order based on what the audience needs to understand, not on the chronology of the animation.

  4. 04 / Delivery

    Explain

    Show what changed, what remains uncertain, and where the visitor can inspect the underlying material.
